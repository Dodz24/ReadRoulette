## The problem, in one sentence
- Manga and manhwa readers spend ten or more minutes endlessly scrolling through ranking sites, genre lists, and social media without being able to decide on a single title to read.
## Who it is for
- This app is for manga and manhwa readers who read several titles a month and know the general genre or format they are in the mood for, but need a quick, focused single-title recommendation rather than scrolling through overwhelming lists or asking for suggestions on Discord and Reddit.
